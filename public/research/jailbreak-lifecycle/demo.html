Jailbreak Lifecycle Explorer

Companion to the paper "A public jailbreak within days, failure reports within hours: chat-model jailbreaks on a public forum, 2023 to 2025" by Preet Ghanshyam Dave. Built from public metadata only: release dates (Epoch AI), post and comment timestamps from r/ChatGPTJailbreak via the Arctic Shift archive (bot and moderator template comments removed), and Hugging Face repository names. No jailbreak text is included.

How long a shared jailbreak lasts

Share of shared jailbreaks with a comment reporting that they are patched or no longer work, by days since posting. Curves are Kaplan-Meier estimates, an upper estimate: most posts stop getting comments within a day, and the estimate assumes quiet posts would have drawn reports like the ones still discussed. The text under the chart also gives the share actually observed within 7 days (a lower bound). Pick up to three groups; hover the chart to read every curve at one time.

Failure reports over time

Weekly share of human forum comments that report a jailbreak failing. Dashed lines mark vendor events; hover one for its 14-day change.

Open weights: days to a safety-stripped copy

Time from each open model family's release to the first Hugging Face repository with "abliterated" or "uncensored" in its name. Upper bound: copies under other names are not counted.

Risk calculator

The paper's Cox model (all posting-time features). It ranks posts only modestly better than chance on held-out data (C-index shown below), and its absolute risks are not well calibrated (its integrated Brier score is worse than a no-feature baseline), so read the output as a base rate with small adjustments, not a forecast for one post.
