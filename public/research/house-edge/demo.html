How bookmakers and casinos keep winning: the price they charge, the products they steer money into and the customers they choose to serve. Every number comes from the paper's code, run on football matches (football-data.co.uk, 2000/01 to 2025/26).

3. People: margin versus limiting winners

A book prices every match at Bet365's odds rescaled to overround m and takes bets from six kinds of customer. If it limits accounts, it watches each one and cuts the stake of anyone whose bets beat the closing price (or show a profit) by more than z standard errors after 30 bets. Expected values are measured against Pinnacle's closing price. The customer mix is an assumption you can change. Profit is per unit of handle offered before limiting; hold divides by the handle the book accepts, so it rises when limited handle leaves.

Archetypes: favourite backers, longshot bettors (odds 5 or more), 4-fold favourite accumulators, random picks, value bettors (book price beats Pinnacle's pre-closing fair price by 2%) and a LightGBM model bettor (edge over 5%). The model bettor loses on average, so it counts as recreational; the sharp share is the value bettors' share of handle before limiting.
