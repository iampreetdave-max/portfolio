Interactive demo

Dataset discovery benchmark explorer

120 requests for open data, answered by the Open Data Finder (a small model that may only pick datasets that eight catalog APIs returned, every link fetched) and by two LLMs with web search. Every link was checked when the answer came back. The 102 held-out needs are the primary set; the 18 needs used while building the finder are shown separately as development needs. The finder is live at tensortapri.org/data.
