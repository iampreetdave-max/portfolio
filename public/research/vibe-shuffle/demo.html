How it works

Every song in the library plays exactly once; nothing is added. The sampler targets a distribution with probability proportional to exp(−cost / T): a fast sequential draw followed by 20 rounds of local swaps, which approximates that target without sampling it exactly. The cost adds up how far each step moves in valence and energy and across genre families, minus a bonus for moves you like, plus an optional pull toward the arc. "Never" moves and the same artist twice within two songs are hard rules. The surprise slider sets T: far left is close to the smoothest order, far right is close to a random order among those your rules allow. With the Gentle rules that is still smoother than a plain shuffle; choose Anything goes for a plain one.
